“The Aqueduct of Segovia (Spanish: [Acueducto de Segovia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acueducto_de_Segovia)) is a Roman aqueduct in Spain, built around the first century AD to channel water from springs in the mountains 17 kilometres (11 mi) to Segovia's fountains, public baths and private houses, in use until 1973.”
This place was pretty impressive, when you think about when it was built, and how long it was in use.
