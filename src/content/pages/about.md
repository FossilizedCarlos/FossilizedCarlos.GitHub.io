## What’s the difference between Blog and Notes?

I really liked [Mara's](https://dataand.me/about#whats-the-difference-between-blog-and-notes) definition for these sections, so borrowed it for my own site[^1][^2], but also the overall look of her about page.

> [**Blog**](https://dataand.me/blog) posts are (mainly) static. I write them, publish them, and don’t typically go back and update them. If I’m doing it right, they should be helpful to other people.
>
> I keep “living”-ish documents on things I’m learning in [**Notes**](https://dataand.me/notes). The primary audience for notes is me. They’re likely to change as I learn more, and might not be all that helpful for other folks. These days, I stash most of my notes in a Quarto book aptly named [**Mara’s Notebook**](https://notes.maraaverick.com/).

## Micro Blogs and POSSE

I was not aware of the [POSSE](https://indieweb.org/POSSE) ("Publish [on your] Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere") model until I saw Molly White's [toot](https://hachyderm.io/@molly0xfff/112068291411548302), which was also published on her [site](https://www.mollywhite.net/micro/entry/202403091817), and syndicated to different services automatically.<br />
The Micro section is my attempt at creating a micro blog and syndicating to Mastodon, Bluesky, and Micro.blog. If you want to comment on any blog, note, or micro post, please do so at your social site of choice.

## What tools are used to make this site?

This website is written in Markdown using [Nova](https://nova.app) and [iA Writer](https://ia.net/writer), built with [Astro](https://astro.build), hosted on [Github Pages](https://pages.github.com), and uses [AstroPaper](https://astro.build/themes/details/astropaper/) theme with a few modifications to fit my needs.

## Why did you choose that theme?

Originally, this site was being updated on [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) using the [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page/) theme, but in one of my many Claude adventures I updated my other site using Astro and immdeiately decided to have both use Astro. AstroPaper is a simple and nice looking theme, which resonates with me. I will tweak it a little here and there, but overall love that type writer look to it!
